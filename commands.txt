 When run.status == 'AWAITING_REVIEW' and the pending gate is clip_approval_gate, the Next.js frontend must render the clip editor:
   - Trim timeline (start_sec / end_sec via PATCH /api/clips/candidates/{id}/)
   - Crop / area selection (PATCH layout/)
   - Watermark, captions, intro, outro, music (PATCH style/)
   - Timed overlays
   - "Approve" button → POST /api/runs/{run_id}/candidates/approve-all/ (marks candidates)
   - "Start Render" button → POST /api/runs/{run_id}/start-render/ with {approved_candidate_ids: [...]} (optional; auto-collects APPROVED candidates when omitted)
